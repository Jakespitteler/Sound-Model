# 003 — Measure background noise and relevant weather

Status: todo. Priority: P0. Owner: unassigned.
Dependencies: 001.

## Work

Plan representative town measurements for relevant hours, seasons and operating states. Record meter calibration, positions/heights, averaging/statistical metrics, raw evidence and meteorology. Separate existing mine activity from unrelated ambient contributions where possible.

Collect wind direction/speed, temperature/stability or profiles, humidity and representative adverse propagation conditions. Cloud cover alone cannot determine acoustic refraction. Agree data quality and completeness with the acoustic reviewer.

## Acceptance evidence

A documented measurement plan and quality-checked dataset support background comparison and validation; missing/contaminated periods are marked. No universal cloud-to-dB multiplier is introduced.
