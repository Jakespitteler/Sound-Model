# 001 — Agree the assessment basis and town receptors

Status: todo. Priority: P0. Owner: unassigned.
Dependencies: none.

## Work

Confirm operating locations, nearby homes and other sensitive receivers, proposed hours and worst credible combinations of machinery. Ask what “disruption” means for this project: applicable legal criteria, community amenity, background-relative changes, sleep disturbance and particular sound character. They are different assessment questions.

For Greenbushes, have a suitably qualified reviewer verify current WA requirements, approval conditions, receiver categories, time periods, metrics and any tonal/impulsive adjustments from authoritative sources. Do not hard-code an arbitrary acceptable dB threshold or equate compliance with inaudibility.

## Acceptance evidence

Record a versioned assessment brief, surveyed receptor coordinates/heights, applicable criteria with source/date/reviewer, and named open questions. A scenario lacking these inputs remains screening-only; it cannot produce an unqualified “safe to operate” result.
