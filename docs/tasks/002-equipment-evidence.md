# 002 — Collect traceable equipment sound-power data

Status: todo. Priority: P0. Owner: unassigned.
Dependencies: 001.

## Work

Add evidence records for measured/manufacturer sound power, octave-band spectra, uncertainty, operating load/mode, measurement standard, calibration, date and source document. Keep sound power and sound pressure explicitly distinct. Record equipment directivity, source height and relevant tonal/impulsive characteristics.

Review unusual values rather than treating the 300 dB software limit as a plausible machine specification. 300 dB re 1 pW is 10^18 W acoustic power, an extreme numerical input, not a recommended machinery value.

## Acceptance evidence

Users can distinguish illustrative and evidenced equipment data, trace every input to its document, and identify missing spectra/uncertainty. Import validation prevents silent pressure-to-power substitution or inconsistent units.
