Model assumptions & limits

Independent point sources in a full-space free field. Levels combine energetically. No ground reflection, terrain, buildings, barriers, wind, temperature or atmospheric absorption. This can underestimate real levels.

Distances below 1 m use a 1 m display limit and are flagged. The overlay samples a 48 × 48 grid; receiver values are calculated directly. Zoom in for detail.

Sound power data and background measurements are needed for a defensible audibility assessment. Street mapping is a temporary fallback; satellite imagery is not yet included.
