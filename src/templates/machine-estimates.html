Where the starter machine levels came from

These are project-authored guesses, dated 17 November 2026. They are not manufacturer specifications, site measurements, published company figures or verified fleet data.

The eight starter categories are representative of open-pit mining, earthmoving and ore processing: large haul truck, hydraulic excavator, wheel loader, tracked dozer, motor grader, water cart, blast-hole drill and crusher/screening plant. They are plausible scenario-building choices for work associated with Charles Hull and Talison Lithium, not a claim about either company's actual fleet or particular machine models.

Each original number and assumption note is recorded under Scenario → Machine data → Estimate notes & edit. The assumed sound-power levels range from 112 to 120 dB(A) LwA. They are rough starting values chosen for exploring the app; no statistical uncertainty or conservatism is implied. You can replace both the number and its note.

Use the right acoustic quantity

Machine inputs are A-weighted sound power, LwA, referenced to 1 pW. Receiver readings and the humorous sound-level guide are sound-pressure levels, referenced to 20 µPa. Do not copy those guide numbers into the machine catalogue as sound power.

Replace these guesses with measured or manufacturer sound-power evidence, operating conditions and appropriate spectra before using a scenario for a town-noise assessment. The current free-field model also lacks terrain, barriers and meteorological propagation effects.
