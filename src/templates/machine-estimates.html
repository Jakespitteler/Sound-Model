Equipment example levels

The eight starter types use rough sound-power estimates of 112–120 dB(A) LwA. They are examples for trying a scenario, not verified manufacturer or site measurements.

In Machine data, replace a level with your equipment's sound-power value and keep its source or operating assumptions in the note. Sound pressure measured beside a machine is a different quantity.

The starter types represent common mining and earthmoving equipment; they do not identify a confirmed Charles Hull or Talison fleet. Original estimates and dates remain in each type's note.
