Model assumptions & limits

Independent point sources in a full-space free field. Contours use adaptive, source-centred Marching Squares so panning or zooming never reloads the model.

Levels combine energetically for independent machines. Lines are approximate level sets, not phase-cancellation fringes. Drag a line or its labelled handle to set its threshold at the drop point; its shape is always recalculated.

No ground reflection, terrain, buildings, barriers, wind, temperature or atmospheric absorption are included.
