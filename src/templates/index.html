Check the noise at town

  1. Start with a machine or a meter reading. Use Machines to place equipment from the catalogue. Use Meter readings to record the level, location and test conditions.
  2. Build an estimate from field readings. Choose one isolated machine or mark a whole area. Use two readings to build the estimate and a different one to check it. Review the result before applying.
  3. Check a listening point. In Receivers, choose Add listening point and click the location you want to check. Expand it to see each source’s contribution.
  4. Compare and save. Adjust the Contours or place extra machines that were absent during your test. Select Save to keep your scenario.
